About NĀRO

Built to help installers win the revenue they have already earned.

Personal, direct, and without the noise. This is where NĀRO came from and where it is going.

NĀRO was built by Joshua Manusama, out of the conviction that installation companies leave too much revenue on the table.

Not because of poor work or prices that are too high, but simply because quotes are left sitting. A quote goes out, no reply comes back, and then it disappears into the rush of the day. No time to chase it. No system that takes it over.

That is exactly what we solve.

NĀRO Close is the entry point.

It is the first module of NĀRO OS, an operating system that automates the full client cycle for technical service providers. From quote to follow-up, from appointment to invoice: step by step, without you having to overhaul the way you work.

Close starts at the moment that delivers the most return: the quotes you have already sent. Automatic, personal follow-up on day 3, 7, and 14, written in your voice, from your own name.

Direct, honest, and backed by a guarantee.

We believe in results, not in long contracts. Every tier comes with a 60-day guarantee. If you get no reply at all to your follow-up emails in that period, we continue at no cost until the first reply comes in (max. 60 extra days). Condition: a minimum of 5 quotes per month through the system.
