Frequently asked questions

Does this work with our current quoting software?
NĀRO Close connects automatically to your existing email environment. No new software, no manual setup. Ready within 30 minutes.
Will my client see that the email was sent automatically?
No. The follow-up comes from your own email address, with your name and signature. To your client, it's indistinguishable from an email you wrote yourself.
What if a client replies earlier?
NĀRO Close automatically stops following up as soon as a reply comes in. No duplicate emails, no awkward moments.
How fast am I live?
Within 48 hours of agreeing to start. Onboarding is a single 30-minute call.
Is there a contract?
Yes, a short service agreement. Pilot: minimum 2 months, then monthly cancellable or a new 3-month block. Founding and Partner: minimum 3 months, then monthly cancellable or a new 3-month block.
How does the 60-day guarantee work?
If the Service gets zero email replies to your follow-ups within 60 days, we continue at no cost until the first reply comes in (max. 60 extra days). Condition: minimum 5 quotes per month through the system. Results are measured via the email channel.
